# Maliwan 1.0 Product Vision

Maliwan is a LINE-based household assistant designed to help senior family members and caregivers with practical daily care tasks.

The first version focuses on simple LINE interactions for:

- household inventory
- meal planning
- medication tracking

The product value is not to build a general chatbot.
It is to make daily household care easier through:

- clear menus
- low-typing interaction
- reliable task flows
